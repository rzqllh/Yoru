# Watch Guide: Relationships, Release Years and Story Chronology

The guide uses AniList relationships and selected editorial routes. **A title relationship does not determine the order of in-story events.** A two-hop graph is a bounded discovery aid, not a complete franchise canon.

## Two explicit timeline modes

- **Tahun rilis** (available): sort mapped entries using known year/month metadata; undated entries appear last. This is a release-metadata view, not a promise of preferred watch order.
- **Kronologi** (evidence-gated): enabled only when a separate source-reviewed editorial route explicitly classifies its steps as in-story chronology and passes validation. **Currently unavailable for bundled routes.** A recommendation, alternative, or broadcast-order route is not relabelled as chronology.

The UI intentionally has no third global timeline mode.

## Curated routes and editions

Source-backed suggested watch routes currently cover a bounded set of Fate, Monogatari, Haruhi and Re:ZERO examples. Fate branches are alternatives, Monogatari is an introductory editorial route, Haruhi 2006 and 2009 have different episode numbering, and Re:ZERO distinguishes two OVAs under one metadata entry. These examples preserve their edition and spoiler qualifications. They do not establish a complete franchise or a universal chronological canon.

A route may include optional steps, alternative branches and episode ranges; numbering belongs **only to the named edition**. A detail link is not a verified streaming episode link. Manual completion is user-managed and does not imply that playback occurred.

## Editorial evidence requirements

For each newly added route require: stable route identifier, exact anime IDs, dated HTTPS citations, review date, edition and episode numbering where needed, spoiler risks, optionality, and a clear statement of incomplete coverage. Disputed or unverified chronology should remain disabled. Source links should be checked before publication.

## Browse safely

Use relationship filters for TV, movie, extra, alternative branch, recap and other media. Request a second relationship depth explicitly; fan-out is bounded and API errors/partial results are disclosed. A franchise can have multiple continuities; do not infer a single timeline from prequel/sequel edges.

Submit corrections with exact title/edition and verifiable primary or authoritative publisher sources in [Issue #44](https://github.com/rzqllh/Yoru/issues/44). Source availability and anime metadata are independent from editorial order.
